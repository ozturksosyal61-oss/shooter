The city fell. The rescue chopper promised over the radio never arrives. You are the last soldier standing — keep patrolling!

HOLDOUT: LAST CITY is a fast, punchy top-down zombie shooter built for touch. Move on the left, hold and drag on the right to aim and fire. Vault over cover, slip through the horde and hold out as the waves grow.

AN ENDLESS CITY
• Districts that change as you walk: avenues, bazaar, industrial zone and harbour
• Parks, plazas, alleys, gas stations, police stations and hospitals

GIANT MUTANTS
• Four original bosses born from a lab accident: Butcher, Carrion, Plague Mother and Doombringer

EVERY RUN IS DIFFERENT

GEAR UP
